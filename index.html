Streamis

A desktop app (Windows) for live streamers. It gathers the chat, donations and alerts of a stream into one place and shows them as overlays in OBS.

Works with YouTube

Streamis uses YouTube API Services. A streamer connects their own YouTube channel with Google sign-in, and Streamis then:

  • shows the live chat of the streamer's current YouTube broadcast next to the Twitch chat;
  • lets the streamer and their chat bot reply in the YouTube live chat and run chat commands and moderation (the streamer's own channel only);
  • shows the live viewer count, new subscribers and Super Chats on the stream overlay;
  • shows the streamer's own channel statistics inside the app.

All data stays on the streamer's own computer. Streamis has no servers and no user accounts of its own.

Who it is for

Individual live streamers who broadcast on Twitch and YouTube at the same time. Each streamer installs the app on their own PC and connects only their own channels.
