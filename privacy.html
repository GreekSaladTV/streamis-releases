Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Streamis is a desktop application for live streamers. This policy explains what data Streamis accesses, how it is used, where it is stored and how you can remove it.

YouTube API Services

Streamis uses YouTube API Services. By connecting YouTube in Streamis you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Your data is also handled according to the Google Privacy Policy.

What data Streamis accesses

When you sign in with Google and connect your YouTube channel, Streamis requests these permissions:

  • youtube.force-ssl — to read the live chat of your current broadcast, send chat messages on your behalf (replies, chat commands, messages of your chat bot), and moderate your own live chat (for example, time out a user or add a moderator) when you ask it to;
  • yt-analytics.readonly — to show statistics of your own channel inside the app.

Through these permissions Streamis reads: your channel name and statistics; your live broadcasts and their viewer counts; messages in your live chat (author display name, channel ID, message text, Super Chat amounts); the list of your recent subscribers; and your own videos and their statistics.

Streamis does not access your email address, contacts, payment data, or any channel other than the ones you connect.

How the data is used

The data is used only to provide the app's features to you: show your chat and alerts in the app and on your stream overlay, count viewers and chat activity, run the chat commands and chat bot you configure, and show your channel statistics. Streamis does not sell data, does not use it for advertising and does not build profiles of users.

Where the data is stored

Streamis has no servers. Everything is stored locally on your own computer, in the app's data folder:

  • Google sign-in tokens are encrypted with your operating system's secure storage (Windows DPAPI).
  • Chat messages, events and statistics are kept in a local database on your computer, with local backups in the same folder.

The developer of Streamis cannot access this data.

How long YouTube data is kept

  • Data received from YouTube API Services is kept for no longer than 30 days. Streamis checks this when it starts and every 6 hours after that.
  • After 30 days, viewer names, chat and Super Chat message texts, and the original API responses are deleted. Only the amount, currency, type and date of each Super Chat or membership event are kept, as statistics of your own channel. Chat activity of YouTube viewers is reduced to anonymous daily counts (how many people wrote and how many messages), and video statistics in the content plan are deleted after 30 days.
  • Statistics are kept only while Streamis is still authorized to access your channel. Streamis checks this authorization every 6 hours.
  • The app's log files and local database backups are also cleared of entries older than 30 days.

When data leaves your computer

Streamis sends data only to the services needed for the features you turn on:

  • Google / YouTube — requests to YouTube API Services on your behalf.
  • AI chat bot (optional, off by default) — if you enable it and enter your own API key, the text of the chat message it answers and the author's display name are sent to the AI provider you choose (Anthropic, OpenAI, OpenRouter or xAI) to generate a reply.
  • Text-to-speech (optional) — if you enable voice alerts, the text to be read aloud (for example a donation or Super Chat message) is sent to Microsoft's speech service.

No other third parties receive your data. Streamis does not use cookies or analytics trackers.

How to revoke access and delete data

  • Disconnect YouTube in Streamis (Sources → YouTube → Full sign-out). Streamis immediately revokes its access token with Google and deletes the stored tokens and all YouTube data from your computer.
  • Revoke Streamis's access to your Google account at any time at Google security settings (security.google.com/settings/security/permissions). If you revoke access there and do not connect YouTube again, Streamis deletes all YouTube data from your computer 14 days after the revocation. If Streamis is not running at that moment, it deletes the data as soon as it starts.
  • Delete all local data by uninstalling Streamis and removing its data folder (%APPDATA%\streamis on Windows).
